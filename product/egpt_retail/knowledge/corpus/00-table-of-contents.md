## Corpus Sourcing Note (Training Corpus)

This `research_docs/` corpus is designed for **SME reference** and **SLM training**, so **verifiable citations are preferred for all quantitative claims**. In a limited number of places, you may see figures labeled as an **“approximate benchmark”** (historically phrased as “per industry estimates”). These are intended as *directional heuristics* used for planning and magnitude checks, and they are **not independently verified** within this corpus unless a specific report/publisher/year is cited alongside the figure.

If you use this corpus for model training or downstream decision support, treat **approximate benchmarks** as *non-authoritative defaults* and replace them with **primary sources** (report name, publisher, year) where possible.
